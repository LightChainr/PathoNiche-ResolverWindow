# Methods audit summary

This release distinguishes documented analysis facts from chronology-based inference and from information absent in the archive.

## Corrections incorporated

1. The four-part prediction framework is reported as the final analytical organization. The archive does not support prospective preregistration of the final wording.
2. GSE305331 comprises seven pooled condition libraries, each pooling three mice. Its 543,639 nuclei are technical observations and the timing display is descriptive.
3. UQ geometry and biopsy registration preceded module-score generation in the archived run. Strict operator blinding to ECM, retention, and repair information is not documented.
4. Figure 3 no longer places HSC weighted evidence tallies and macrophage effect-derived values on one numeric scale.
5. CODEX has one section at each displayed stage; its three estimates are presented independently without a stage-trend line.
6. The human two-component analysis is a static full-covariance Gaussian-mixture model. Potential, flow, and hysteresis language has been removed.
7. The LINCS forward-reverse difference follows from an asymmetric role-weighted formula. It nominates a factorial experiment and does not estimate an observed order effect.
8. Safety and computational annotations were not enforced by the archived pair-generation script and are not described as hard gates.
