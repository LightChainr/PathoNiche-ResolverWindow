# Public raw-data accessions

Large public raw data are intentionally not redistributed. Reconstruct them from the original repositories and cite the source studies.

## GEO

- GSE295293: replicated control, peak-fibrosis, and withdrawal mouse liver single-cell data.
- GSE305331: pooled cross-etiology CCl4 and FAT-MASH withdrawal series.
- GSE261829: Foz/Foz MASH regression macrophage system.
- GSE244832: human NORMAL, MASL, and MASH snRNA-seq/snATAC-seq.
- GSE136103: human healthy and cirrhotic liver single-cell atlas.
- GSE278662: human liver Visium sections used for reaction-front sensitivity analyses.
- GSE48325 and GSE48452: bariatric-surgery methylation/expression resources used for paired sensitivity analyses.
- GSE33650, GSE135251, and GSE162694: external human bulk or biopsy-stage resources.
- GSE305330 and GSE306327: pooled or spatial withdrawal resources retained in Extended Data analyses.
- GSE92742: LINCS L1000 perturbation compendium used for genetic and compound-signature analyses.

GEO landing-page pattern: `https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=ACCESSION`
